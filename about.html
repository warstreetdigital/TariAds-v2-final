Marketplace Trust Principles

To ensure safe transactions when buying, renting, or viewing listings, remember these core rules:

  • Never pay viewing or reservation fees in advance. Legitimate landlords, dealers, and sellers never demand payment before you have physically inspected the property or vehicle in daylight.
  • Communicate directly with confirmed listers. Use the WhatsApp button to verify contact details, ask questions, and confirm exact GPS locations.
  • Inspect documentation in person. Verify title deeds, vehicle logbooks, and product warranty cards prior to executing lease agreements or bank payments.
